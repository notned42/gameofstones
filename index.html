Seven Stones — Rules of the Game

1. The Bag & The Stones

The velvet bag starts each round filled with exactly 15 stones: 7 Orange, 7 Blue, and 1 Green. Stones are drawn randomly without replacement during the round (15 → 14 → 13...). When a new round starts, the bag resets to 15 stones.

2. Turns & Bidding

You begin each round with 7 Purple Stones (unless an achievement bonus grants more). Each round lasts a maximum of 10 turns. Each turn, bid at least 1 stone on Orange (2x payout), Blue (2x payout), or Green (15x payout). Wrong guesses lose the bid.

3. Lifetime Scoring Formula

Score = ((X² · P) - (X² · P · 0.5·G) + 7·T + 7·F) · (S^(3/7))

Where X = turns / 10, P = final purple stones, G = 1 if you busted on a green bid, T = turns survived, F = 1 if you finished all 10 turns, and S = your streak multiplier. Multiplier Rule: S equals 1 until you achieve a streak of at least 3 correct consecutive same-round guesses for the same color (if streak ≥ 3, S = streak; otherwise S = 1).

4. Achievements & Persistence

24 unique trophies exist. Achievement bonuses (flat points & multipliers) are applied immediately to your persistent lifetime score, saved across browser sessions!
