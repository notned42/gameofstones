Rules & Dynamic Payouts

Every draw alters the composition of the bag. Study the rules below.

01

Starting Bag Composition

The bag starts with exactly 15 stones: 7 Orange, 7 Blue, and 1 Green. Outside the bag, you begin with 7 Purple Stones in your bankroll.

02

Payout Multipliers

Guess Orange correctly to receive 2x your purple bet. Guess Blue correctly to receive 2x. Guess the elusive Green to receive 15x in return!

03

Stone Depletion

After each play, the drawn stone is left out of the bag. When an orange stone is drawn, only 6 orange remain, shifting the odds for future turns.

The Mathematics of Stone Depletion

Unlike roulette or coin tosses, every stone removed shifts the probability distribution for the next draw. When one color is repeatedly drawn, the unpicked colors become increasingly probable.

How to Play Stone Bag

1. Bag Composition: The bag holds 15 stones (7 orange, 7 blue, 1 green). Each has equal chance of being drawn.

2. Your Wallet: You start with 7 purple stones outside the bag.

3. Wager & Multipliers:
• Pick Orange: 2x return on bet
• Pick Blue: 2x return on bet
• Pick Green: 15x return on bet

4. Stones Stay Out: Once drawn, the stone is removed permanently until you reset or the bag empties. Odds recalculate on every single play!
